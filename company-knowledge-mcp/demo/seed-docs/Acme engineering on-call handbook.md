# Acme Engineering — On-call handbook (excerpt)

## Public holidays
On-call over a public holiday is voluntary and paid at 1.5× the standard on-call rate. Swaps must be recorded in the rota by the Thursday before.

## Escalation
SEV-1: page the incident commander immediately. SEV-2: page the owning team's on-call; incident commander is optional. Post an incident channel within 15 minutes for SEV-2 and above.

## Postmortems
Every SEV-2 or higher gets a postmortem within five working days, owned by the on-call engineer at the time of the incident. Postmortems are blameless.
